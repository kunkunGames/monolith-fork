## 1. Scope

Add `mesh.replace_static_mesh_geometry_in_place` as a conservative action for copying every committed donor StaticMesh `MeshDescription` LOD into an already-existing target while retaining the exact object/package path. The supported surface is intentionally narrow: non-Nanite, no HiRes data, stable committed render data, explicit named/remapped material-slot metadata or a separately selected strict single-slot policy, exact section topology, authored simple primitives with `UseSimpleAsComplex` or `UseSimpleAndComplex`, no external complex mesh, and an exact positive numbered changelist. `UseSimpleAndComplex` rebuilds its derived complex collision from the copied geometry while preserving authored `UBodySetup` state.
