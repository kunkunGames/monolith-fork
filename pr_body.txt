- 💡 What:
  Added `.Reserve()` calls to 10 instances of `TArray<TSharedPtr<FJsonValue>>` and 1 `TSet` before sequential `.Add()` operations in `MonolithAudioAssetActions.cpp`, `MonolithLogicDriverAssetActions.cpp`, `MonolithLogicDriverComponentActions.cpp`, and `MonolithLogicDriverTextGraphActions.cpp`.

- 🎯 Why:
  Sequential `.Add()` calls without a prior `.Reserve()` trigger unnecessary heap allocations and array re-copying as the array dynamically resizes. Pre-allocating the correct capacity avoids this waste.

- 📊 Impact:
  Static/algorithmic estimate: Prevents small heap allocations per execution of several actions that build JSON arrays.

- 🔬 Measurement:
  Not available (UE/editor benchmark unavailable). proxy metric: fewer allocations.

- 🔁 Safety:
  This change preserves behavior exactly. It solely adjusts the internal initial capacity of `TArray` instances before items are added, which is a standard Unreal Engine idiom that does not alter data shape, types, or logging.
